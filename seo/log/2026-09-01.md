and 20.8 for `invoice.payment_failed`, 19 impressions, no clicks. The title was

## Needs Bruce

1. **The comparison in Step 2 does not work at a weekly cadence.** The fetch
   pulls a 28 day window, so two runs a week apart share 26 days and every delta
   is noise. Three options, and this run has no basis to pick one:
   (a) run the cycle monthly; (b) keep the cycle weekly but fetch a 7 day window
   so consecutive files do not overlap, accepting much smaller numbers;
   (c) keep the 28 day window and only compare files four or more weeks apart,
   which makes Step 2's comparison a monthly step inside a weekly routine.
   Until this is settled, "pages that lost position" in Step 2 is not a real
   signal and should not drive a change.
2. **PR #3 has been open since 31 August and PR #4 is now behind it.** Both touch
   `docs/blog/index.html` and `docs/sitemap.xml`, which are generated, so whichever
   merges second may conflict there. The fix is a rebuild, not a hand merge.
3. **`app.recoverflow.org` is in the index**, 7 impressions, and it surfaces on
   the brand query `recoverflow` at position 10 against the marketing site's 7.1.
   The application competing with the marketing site on its own brand name is not
   what anyone wants. It is outside `docs/`, so this routine cannot fix it: it
   needs a `noindex` header or a robots rule on that deployment.
