## The finding: the cycle is designed weekly and fires daily

`ROUTINE.md` is headed "Weekly SEO cycle" and Step 3 reasons about "a weekly cycle". The
scheduler entry `recoverflow-seo-optimization-routine` is `0 10 * * *`, every day. Seven
runs happened in the nine days to today and all seven read the same Search Console file.

"considered and rejected" section is now longer than the "changed" section in every log,
